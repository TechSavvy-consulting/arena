TechSavvy Arena complete campaign rebuild v0.11

All eighteen rebuilt creature/guardian bodies and all five first-person guns,
gloved hands and sleeves were authored locally with Blender 4.5.14 LTS using
the retained reproducible tools/build_realism_*.py scripts and original detail
maps. No models were extracted from another commercial game or purchased.
The six maps retain original gameplay topology and receive the reviewed original
industrial detail library, authored weathering and stage-specific set dressing.
See the environment provenance and existing ART_SOURCES_v09.txt for reused
CC0 Poly Haven materials/HDRI and their exact sources/license links.

Kyle's authorized dimensional photo-referenced expression heads remain exposed.
They are not facial scans or a fully rigged anatomical likeness. Original private
source photographs are not distributed. TechSavvy branding used with permission.

The two approved character-gallery images are exact, hash-verified copies of
the existing locally saved generated cast posters. They are whole images, not
repainted, cropped or relabelled. Runtime name keys appear beneath them.
See assets/character-gallery/PROVENANCE.txt for exact SHA-256 identities.
They are illustrations, not evidence of equivalent rendered gameplay fidelity.

Music has six original arrangements using authored motifs, chords, rhythms,
sound design and the previously verified isolated CC0 instrument recordings.
No precomposed song or commercial soundtrack is included. See AUDIO_SOURCES.txt,
the v0.11 music notices and the retained original composition script/manifest.
