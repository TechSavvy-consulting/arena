TechSavvy Arena - Realism Preview art sources
October 15, 2026

The new creature bodies, first-person rifle/shotgun/hands and industrial prop
modules are original models authored locally with Blender 4.5.14 LTS and the
reproducible tools/build_realism_*.py scripts. They are not extracted commercial
game assets and were not generated by uploading private photographs elsewhere.
Authored creature/weapon detail maps are original deterministic texture work.

The existing dimensional photo-referenced Kyle expression heads and TechSavvy
branding are retained with Kyle Hearn's authorization. These are not scanned or
fully facial-rigged human heads. Private full-resolution source photographs are
not included in the portable game package.

Existing CC0 Poly Haven concrete/metal/rock maps and Industrial Sunset 02 HDRI
are reused. See ART_SOURCES_v09.txt for exact source links and license notices.
Existing font, Godot, original music and recorded CC0 sound notices are retained.
