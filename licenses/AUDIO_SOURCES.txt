TechSavvy Arena v0.8 audio provenance
Verified October 14, 2026. No commercial songs or purchased sound packs.

ORIGINAL MUSIC AND DESIGNED EFFECTS
Three original offline adaptive compositions: Reactor Techno (100 BPM, 57.6s),
Dark Synth (80 BPM, 48s), Drum & Bass (150 BPM, 51.2s). Original note patterns,
arrangements, synthesis, fills and processing combine with the five expressly
licensed isolated instrument recordings enumerated below. No borrowed songs,
commercial-song recordings or precomposed musical phrases/loops are used.
Source: tools/build_music_v08.py. Runtime: assets/audio/score-v08/*_*.ogg.
Four phase-matched stereo Vorbis stems play at a time; compressed cache is bounded.
The retained historical v0.7 all-synth source tracks are not used by the v0.8 game.
The shock blaster, ion lance and singularity launcher use original layered
transient/chassis/energy/body/tail synthesis in scripts/arena_audio.gd.
These designed sci-fi effects are not represented as firearm recordings.
Critical and miniboss-alert feedback and the finite victory fanfare are original
non-voice synthesized cues. No voice cloning or imitation of a commercial song.

PREPARATION
tools/prepare_recorded_audio.ps1 explicitly selects/filters/trims these sources,
converts to 22,050 Hz 16-bit mono PCM, levels and fades them for game playback.
Original source files are unchanged. The full 194MB archive is not distributed.
No executables or downloaded scripts from the recording archive are used.
