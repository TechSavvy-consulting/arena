All note/chord progressions, melodic motifs, rhythmic arrangements, fills,
velocity/timing variation, harmonics, synthesis and stereo effects were authored
locally in tools/build_music_v011.py. No commercial song, copyrighted melody,
precomposed musical loop, voice or voice clone was used. No purchases/downloads
or new external services were required for this update.

The five isolated drum/bass/guitar recordings previously acquired and licensed
for v0.8 are reused without modifying the source files. Full source locations,
author credits, verified license text and original SHA256 are preserved in
AUDIO_SOURCES.txt and AUDIO_CC0.txt. These are CC0 Karoryfer instrument samples,
not songs. Exact source and output hashes, uncompressed peaks/RMS, durations and
tempos are recorded in assets/audio/score-v011/manifest.json.

Four phase-matched stereo Vorbis stems per arrangement: pulse, percussion,
bass and arpeggio/harmony. Original warm pads, string/brass-like synthesizers,
plucks, bells, sub/reese bass, crash/tom/clap/metal transients combine with the
isolated recorded instruments. These are not represented as a recorded live
orchestra. 24-to-40-bar structures include restrained introductions, changes,
breakdowns, returns and section fills rather than an eight-second phrase.

Generation uses existing local NumPy and FFmpeg. PCM intermediates/listening
mixes are kept in task-specific Codex Temp. Runtime uses only compressed stems.
Music is protected by the game's dedicated compressor/ducking/limiter chain,
with -1 dBFS ceiling; preferences/mute/focus and independent SFX volume remain.
No Windows audio levels, global sound devices or other applications are changed.
