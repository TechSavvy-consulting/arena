Stay alive & get stronger+

Keep moving. Use rooms, cover and upper paths. Orange floor warnings mean move now; shallow water is traversable, but marked power zones are hazardous.

Break their gear. Shoot exposed heads, mounted weapons, armor and tentacles. Disabling components reduces what the enemy can do.

Recover. Health begins recovering after six seconds without damage. One grenade regenerates every twenty seconds, up to five. Use ammo stations with E or □.

Stack upgrades. Pick one of three choices between waves. All eight benefits have unlimited ranks during the finite campaign. Bigger Magazines increases all five guns' clip capacities; Hot Barrel boosts their damage.

Checkpoints. Clear a wave and select an upgrade to save. Continue resumes the next wave. A new run replaces that checkpoint. Menus and loss of focus pause game clocks.

Five weapons. Six music choices.+

Your arsenal: AR-30 rifle, Breach shotgun, ARC shock blaster, Ion Lance piercer and Singularity launcher. Switch with △ or Tab; upgrade benefits carry across the applicable weapons.

Your soundtrack: Reactor Techno, Dark Synth, Drum & Bass, Neon Drive, Foundry Breaks and Titan Score. Select a style in Settings & controls to hear it immediately. The score adapts to the action. There is no separate demo button.

Pause → Characters opens both Kyle cast posters. Settings includes independent music/SFX volume, sensitivity, deadzone and graphics choices.

PC requirements & troubleshooting+

Windows 10/11, 64-bit. This release uses Godot's Forward+ Vulkan renderer and needs compatible graphics hardware and drivers. Verified on an NVIDIA RTX 4060 Laptop GPU; broad minimum-spec testing is not complete. Not a Mac, phone, browser or console build.

No sound? Bring the game into focus, check the Windows volume mixer and both in-game sliders. Music intentionally stops when the game loses focus.

No controller / double inputs? Confirm DS4Windows shows a connected controller with virtual Xbox 360 output. Start with one controller and one mapper; use F6 if needed. Never install drivers from an unknown download site.

Can't launch? Extract the ZIP first; keep the .exe and .pck together. Update GPU drivers from the manufacturer. The executable is unsigned, so Windows may show a reputation warning. Verify the source/checksum and scan the download; do not disable security protections.

Local, single-player game. Friends download and play on their own PCs. There is no online co-op, account, leaderboard server or telemetry in this website.
